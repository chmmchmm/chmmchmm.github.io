Support · 支持

薮猫科技 (Andsec)

iOS client · com.cyberserval.andsec

This app is issued to you by your own organisation. Most problems are configuration problems on the organisation side, so start there.本应用由你所在的单位为你开通。多数问题其实是客户侧配置问题，请先按下面的顺序排查。

1. Server address format一、服务器地址格式

On first launch the app asks for a server address. It must be a bare host, optionally with a port. Do not include https:// or a path.首次启动时应用要求填写服务器地址。只能填主机名，可带端口；不要带 https:// 前缀，也不要带路径。

  • OK — secure.example.com可以 —— secure.example.com
  • OK — secure.example.com:8443可以 —— secure.example.com:8443
  • OK — 10.20.30.40:8443可以 —— 10.20.30.40:8443
  • Rejected — https://secure.example.com:8443会被拒绝 —— https://secure.example.com:8443

Changing the address logs you out and returns you to the sign-in screen.修改服务器地址会退出登录并回到登录页。

2. Sign-in problems二、登录问题

  1. Confirm the server address with your administrator.先与管理员确认服务器地址。
  2. Confirm your account exists and is not disabled — accounts are created by your administrator, the app has no self-registration.确认账号已创建且未被停用——账号由管理员创建，应用内无法自助注册。
  3. If the app cannot reach the server, check that the device is on the required network (VPN / corporate Wi-Fi).如果连不上服务器，确认设备处于所需网络（VPN / 单位 Wi-Fi）。

3. Notifications permission三、通知权限

The app requests notification permission so that policy changes and alerts can reach you. You can change this later in iOS Settings → 薮猫科技 → Notifications. Denying it does not stop the app from working.应用申请通知权限，以便策略变更与告警能及时送达。之后可在 iOS「设置 → 薮猫科技 → 通知」中修改。拒绝通知不影响使用。
