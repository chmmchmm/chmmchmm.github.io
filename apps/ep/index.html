Everproof: Warranty Vault

Receipts, serials, warranties — offline, on your iPhone

Everproof keeps the things a warranty claim asks for in one place: the receipt, the store, the date, the price, the serial number, and how many days are left. When something breaks, you can put the whole claim pack together in under a minute instead of digging through email and a shoebox.

Everproof 把索赔时会用到的东西收在一处：收据、商店、日期、价格、序列号，还剩多少天。 东西坏了，你能在一分钟之内把整套索赔材料凑齐，而不用去翻邮箱和鞋盒。

Everything stays on your device. Everproof has no account and no server, and it contains no network code. It works on a plane, in a basement, with airplane mode on. Read the privacy policy.

一切都留在你的设备上。Everproof 没有账号也没有服务器，代码里没有任何网络请求。 飞机上、地下室里、开了飞行模式都能用。隐私政策在这里。

What it does

  • Scan a receipt, get a record. Photograph or pick a receipt and Everproof reads the store, date, price and order number out of it on your device, then shows you what it found so you can correct it before saving. It never guesses silently: a field it is not sure about is left blank.
  • Countdowns that matter. Each record shows its return window and its warranty end. Retailer return windows are built in as starting points, which you can change.
  • Claim pack. One tap builds a PDF with the record and your attached proof photos, ready to attach to a claim email or hand to a counter.
  • Search across everything, including the text recognised from your receipt photos.
  • Encrypted vault. Opened with a passcode, or Face ID / Touch ID if you switch it on.
  • 扫一张收据，生成一条记录。拍一张或从相册选一张收据，Everproof 在本机读出商店、日期、价格、订单号， 把读到的东西先给你看，你改完再存。它不会悄悄猜：拿不准的字段就留空。
  • 真正有用的倒计时。每条记录显示退货窗口和保修截止。常用商店的退货天数已内置成起点，你可以改。
  • 索赔包。一键生成 PDF，包含记录和附加的凭证照片，可以直接贴进索赔邮件或递给柜台。
  • 全库搜索，包括从收据照片识别出的文字。
  • 加密保险箱。用密码打开，也可以打开 Face ID / Touch ID。

Getting started

  1. Set a passcode when the app asks. Six characters or more, any characters. Write it down somewhere outside the phone — see the warning below.
  2. Tap Add and photograph your first receipt, or pick one from your photo library.
  3. Check the prefilled fields and save. Do the same for the serial number sticker on the device itself; that is the field most often missing when a claim is refused.
  4. Optionally switch on Face ID / Touch ID and reminders in Settings.
  1. 应用让你设密码时就设。至少 6 个字符，任意字符都行。请把密码记在手机以外的地方——原因见下面的提醒。
  2. 点「添加」，拍你的第一张收据，或者从相册选一张。
  3. 检查预填的字段，然后保存。设备机身上的序列号标签也请一并录入——索赔被拒最常见的原因就是缺这一项。
  4. 可以在「设置」里打开 Face ID / Touch ID 和提醒。
About the passcode, seriously. Your passcode is the only key. It is not stored anywhere, not even in a form that could be reversed. If you forget it, Everproof cannot recover your vault, and neither can I. The app will offer to start a new empty vault, and the old data stays unreadable forever. Backups are protected by the same passcode, so a backup made before you forget it needs the same password.

关于密码，请当真。你的密码是唯一的钥匙，它不存在任何地方，连不可逆的形式都不存。 如果你忘了，Everproof 无法找回，我也无法找回。应用会提出新建一个空的保险箱，旧数据会永久不可读。 备份文件用的也是同一个密码，所以忘记之前做的备份也需要同一个密码才能恢复。

Frequently asked

Why is it free?

It is the whole app, free. No trial, no locked features, no subscription. It also has no ads and no analytics, because it has no network code.

这就是完整的应用，免费。没有试用、没有锁功能、没有订阅。也没有广告和统计，因为它根本没有网络代码。

Can I sync between devices?

No, and that is deliberate — syncing means a server. Use Settings → Backup to write an encrypted archive, then move that file yourself (Files, AirDrop, your own drive) and restore it on the other device.

不能，这是刻意的——同步就意味着要有服务器。用「设置 → 备份」生成一个加密文件， 然后自己把它搬过去（文件、AirDrop、你自己的网盘），在另一台设备上恢复。

What if the scan reads the wrong thing?

Fix it in the editor; nothing is saved until you tap Save. Recognition on a crumpled receipt in poor light can miss a price or misread a digit, so the fields are always editable and anything doubtful is left empty rather than filled with a guess.

在编辑页改掉就行；在你点「保存」之前什么都不会写入。光线差、收据起皱时识别可能漏掉价格或读错数字， 所以字段永远可以改，拿不准的宁可留空也不会填一个猜出来的值。

Does the claim pack prove I bought something?

It is a tidy bundle of your own record plus the proof photos you attached. A retailer decides what it accepts. If you attached no photos, the PDF says so plainly on its face, and every page carries a line saying this is a record entered by a user in an app and is not issued or verified by any retailer or manufacturer.

它是你自己填的记录加上你附加的凭证照片，整理成一份好看的东西。商家接受什么由商家定。 如果你没附照片，PDF 首页就直接写明这一点；每一页页脚都有一行，说明这是用户在应用里录入的记录，不由任何商家或厂商签发或认证。

Can I recover a deleted record?

No. Deleting a record deletes its photos from the device at the same time. If something is worth keeping, export a backup first.

不能。删除一条记录的同时也会把它的照片从设备上删掉。重要的东西请先导出备份。

Does it work without internet?

Yes, always. It has no network code, so there is no failure mode where the server is down.

能，一直都能。它没有网络代码，所以不存在「服务器挂了」这种故障。

Troubleshooting

The camera or photo picker does not open

Go to iOS Settings → Everproof and switch Camera and Photos on. If you denied them the first time, that is the only place to change it.

到 iOS「设置 → Everproof」里打开相机和照片权限。第一次拒绝了的话，只有这里能改。

Face ID stops working after a reboot or a new fingerprint

Expected. The stored key is bound to your current enrollment, so a reboot or a change to your face or fingerprint invalidates it. Unlock with your passcode once and re-enable biometric access in Settings.

这是预期行为。存进去的密钥绑定你当前的录入，所以重启或改了面容/指纹都会让它失效。 用密码解锁一次，再到「设置」里重新打开生物识别即可。

Recognition missed the store name or price

Lay the receipt flat, fill the frame, avoid glare, and take it in daylight. Then type the missing field yourself — a correct field typed by you beats a wrong one read by the app.

把收据摊平、拍满整个画面、避开反光、用日光。然后自己把缺的字段补上—— 你自己填对的字段，比应用读错的强。
