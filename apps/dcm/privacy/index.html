Privacy Policy

DICOM Viewer / DICOM 影像 · Support · com.localdicom.viewer

The short version: DICOM Viewer does not collect, store on any server, transmit, sell or share any personal information or any image data. It has no network permission at all, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Your DICOM files are copied into the app’s own sandbox on your device and are read, decoded and displayed there only.

一句话结论：「DICOM 影像」不收集、不在任何服务器上存储、不传输、不出售、不共享任何个人信息或影像数据。 本 App 完全没有联网权限，因此无法把任何东西发送到任何地方。你的 DICOM 文件被复制进 App 在本机沙盒内，只在你设备上被读取、解码与显示。

Medical images are sensitive data 医学影像属于敏感数据

DICOM files usually contain patient names, ID numbers, birth dates and other health information. Because of that, the design goal of this app is that no such data ever leaves your device — not to a server, not to an ad network, not to a crash reporter. The app also carries the same limits as its display: it is not a medical device, and nothing it shows may be used for diagnosis, treatment or any clinical decision.

DICOM 文件通常包含患者姓名、编号、出生日期等健康信息。因此本 App 的设计目标就是：这些数据永远不离开你的设备—— 不发给服务器、不给广告平台、也不给任何崩溃统计服务。同时 App 也带着与显示结果相同的限制：它不是医疗器械，其显示内容不得用于诊断、治疗或任何临床决策。

Permissions and what they are used for 权限与用途

  • File access (the only one). When you tap import, iOS shows its own file picker and you choose which files or folders the app may read. The app receives access only to what you actively selected, and it copies those files into its own sandbox. It does not scan your device, browse your folders in the background, or read files you did not pick.
  • No network permission. The app cannot reach the internet, so uploading is not possible even in principle.
  • No Photo Library access. The app never reads your photos. When you export a PNG, the iOS share sheet is presented and you decide where that single file goes.
  • No camera, no microphone, no location, no contacts, no HealthKit, no Sign in with Apple. None of these are requested, so none can be used.

文件读取（唯一的权限用途）。点击导入后由 iOS 弹出系统文件选择器，你亲自选择允许 App 读取的文件或文件夹。 App 只获得你主动选择的那部分内容，并把它们复制进自己的沙盒；它不会扫描你的设备、不会在后台浏览你的目录、也不会读取你没选的文件。
没有联网权限。App 无法访问网络，因此从原理上就不可能上传。
不读取相册。App 从不读取你的照片。导出 PNG 时打开的是系统分享面板，这个文件去哪里由你决定。
不使用相机、麦克风、定位、通讯录、健康数据、通过 Apple 登录。这些权限一个都没有申请，因此也不可能被使用。

Where your images live 你的影像存放在哪里

Imported files are copied into the app’s sandbox, which is storage iOS reserves for this app on your device. The library screen shows how many files are stored there. No copy is made on any server, and no copy is sent to the developer. The files you imported into the Files app stay where you put them; the app only keeps its own copies.

导入的文件被复制进 App 的沙盒——那是 iOS 在你设备上划给本 App 的专属存储。图库界面会显示当前存放的文件数量。 任何服务器上都不存在副本，也不会有任何副本发给开发者。你放在「文件」App 里的原件留在原地，App 只保留自己那份副本。

Deleting your data 如何删除数据

  • One series. Delete a series from the library and its files are removed from the sandbox.
  • Everything. Use Delete all images (清空全部影像) in the library to remove every imported file in one action.
  • All of it, including settings. Delete the app from your device. iOS removes the app’s sandbox together with it, so nothing of yours remains.
  • Exported PNGs. Those live wherever you sent them (for example your Photo Library); delete them there. The app keeps no copy of what you exported.
  • No request needed. Because nothing is held by us, there is no data-subject deletion or export request for you to file and for us to answer.

单个序列。在图库里删除某个序列，它的文件就从沙盒中移除。
全部影像。用图库里的「清空全部影像」一次移除所有导入文件。
连同设置一起清空。直接卸载 App，iOS 会连同沙盒一并删除，你的数据不会残留。
已导出的 PNG。它们存在于你发送到的位置（例如照片 App），请到那里删除；App 不保留导出副本。
无需提交请求。由于我们手里没有任何数据，你不必提出删除或导数据的请求，我们也无需处理。

No ads, no tracking, no third parties 无广告、无追踪、无第三方

There is no advertising in the app, no tracking across apps or websites, and no third-party code inside it. Nothing about your usage — which files you opened, which window settings you use, how long you stayed — is recorded or reported anywhere. The App Store “Data Used to Track You” and “Data Linked to You” sections for this app should therefore be empty; if you ever find them populated, that would be a bug worth reporting.

App 内没有广告，没有跨 App 与跨网站的追踪行为，内部也没有任何第三方代码。你的使用情况——打开了哪些文件、用了什么窗口参数、 停留多久——都不会被记录或上报。因此本 App 在 App Store 上的「用于追踪你的数据」与「与你关联的数据」应当都是空的；如果你看到被填上了内容，那是一个值得报告的错误。

Children 儿童隐私

The app collects no data from anyone, so it collects no data from children either — there is nothing to delete, by design. The app is not directed at children and asks for no personal information. If a child’s DICOM study is opened on a device, that file stays inside the app’s sandbox on that device like any other file, and the parent or guardian can remove it with the controls described above.

本 App 不向任何人收集数据，因此也不收集儿童的数据——按设计就没有可删除的东西。App 不以儿童为对象，也不要求提供任何个人信息。 如果设备里打开了儿童的 DICOM 检查文件，它和其他文件一样只留在该设备的 App 沙盒内，家长或监护人可用上文的方式删除。
