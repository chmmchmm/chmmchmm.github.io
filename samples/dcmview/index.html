How the app uses them

The same files ship inside the app bundle. On the first launch the app imports them into its own library, where each series is labelled with a SAMPLE badge and the footer states that they are synthetic. They behave exactly like imported files: they can be opened, windowed, panned, zoomed, inspected tag by tag, and deleted. Once the user deletes them (or clears the library) the app does not put them back.

You can also import them yourself with the Import button in the app, which reads from the iOS Files app — that path is independent of the built-in samples.
