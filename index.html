Apps

Face Shield — Kids Face Blur

Mosaic every child's face in a group photo before you share it.

Face Shield finds faces in your photos and covers them with a mosaic that is re-checked before the photo can be saved. Photos stay in your photo library; nothing is uploaded, and no analytics or third-party SDKs are involved.

分享合影前先把孩子的脸挡住。Face Shield 会找出照片里的人脸并用马赛克覆盖，还会回头复查遮没遮住，遮住才让你存。照片始终留在你的相册里：不上传、无统计、无第三方 SDK。

DICOM Viewer

Open and inspect .dcm files offline, on iPhone.

DICOM Viewer reads DICOM (.dcm) files and decodes the pixel data locally on your iPhone, including compressed transfer syntaxes. It is a viewer for your own files — not a medical device and not for diagnosis.

在 iPhone 上离线打开并查看 .dcm 文件，像素数据也在本机解码，包含常见压缩传输语法。它是给你自己文件用的查看器——不是医疗器械，不用于诊断。

Everproof — Warranty Vault

Sixty seconds to assemble everything a warranty claim asks for.

Everproof keeps receipts, serial numbers, prices and warranty deadlines in one encrypted vault on your iPhone. Photograph a receipt and it reads the store, date and price out of it on the device, then lets you correct what it got wrong. One tap exports a claim pack as a PDF. Free, with no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

Everproof 把收据、序列号、价格和保修截止收进 iPhone 上一个加密保险箱。拍一张收据，它就在本机读出商店、 日期和价格，读错了你可以改。一键把索赔材料导出成 PDF。免费，没有账号、没有服务器、没有统计、没有广告。
